# WATTLINK enclosure assumptions (rancangan, concept)

Every number below was assumed or derived while building the four enclosure models. Items marked as guesses must be measured or verified before anything is printed or powered.
These are renders of a concept design. They are not a tested design.

| Device | Item | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| General | Status | Concept design (rancangan) only. It is not tested, not certified and not safe for mains use. | It needs review by the hardware lead and the supervising lecturer. |
| General | Source of truth | The PDF Aethera_Rancangan Alat is followed for sizes, layout and parts. Where something did not fit, the row below says what was changed. | The PDF gives a 1.5 mm partition, vent slots and a USB-C access hole on the Plug, and the MOV-10D471K. |
| General | Print settings | PETG or ABS, wall 2.5 mm, floor and lid plate 2.5 mm, infill 35 percent, build volume at least 220 x 220 x 250 mm | From the brief. Infill is a slicer setting and is not modeled. |
| General | Lid fit | 0.2 mm per side, rim 3 mm high and 2.5 mm wide | Assumed. The rim drops into the base opening. |
| General | Screws | M3 self-tapping, boss 8 mm wide, pilot hole 2.6 mm, clearance hole 3.4 mm | Assumed. The boss is wide enough to leave a 2.7 mm wall around the pilot hole. |
| General | Cavities | PZEM, ESP32-C3, HLK-PM01 and relay use the cavity sizes of the spec. The DevKit, fuse and MOV use real size plus 1.5 mm. | The spec gives no cavity for the last three, so the same 1.5 mm rule is assumed. |
| General | Mounting | PZEM on 4 standoffs 5 mm wide and 3 mm high. Relay on 2 posts 8 mm wide with a 2.6 mm pilot, hole spacing 44 mm. The DevKit rests on 2 ribs 3 mm high and the SuperMini on 2 ribs 3.8 mm high, both under the short ends of the board. | The relay hole spacing is a guess and must be measured on the real module. The ribs sit at the short ends so they stay clear of the header pin tails. |
| General | Detailed component models | The renders and the STEP assemblies show detailed models. From the KiCad 3D library come the HLK-PM01 body, the ESP32-WROOM-32 module on the DevKit, the relay body, the 2.54 mm pin headers of the DevKit and the SuperMini, and the USB-C receptacle of the SuperMini. The PG7 glands of the Clamp and the Clamp meter come from the mi3nts repository (MIT license). The boards, other connectors, PZEM-004T, ESP32-C3 SuperMini board, OLED, MOV and fuse are simplified models built here from typical sizes. | Public models of the PZEM-004T v3 and the SuperMini exist, but they are basic blocks without headers, so they are not used. The HLK model is the generic HLK-PMxx and measures 34.8 x 20.5 x 15.1 mm. The fit checks still use the plain boxes of the spec sizes. The KiCad models are CC BY-SA 4.0 with a design exception, and the user handles the license. |
| General | PZEM-004T board size | The spec says 73 x 30 x 24 mm. A public simplified model of the v3 board (Duckle29/blog on GitHub, BSD-3-Clause) measures 74.0 x 30.0 x 16.3 mm, and one listing gives a PCB of 74 x 31 mm. | If the real board is 74 x 31 mm, the cavity of 74.5 x 31.5 mm leaves only 0.25 mm per side. That public model does not confirm the 24 mm height of the spec. Measure the real board before printing. |
| General | Pin headers | The DevKit has 2 rows of 19 pins at 2.54 mm pitch, 25.4 mm apart. The SuperMini has 2 rows of 8 pins, 15.24 mm apart. Both use the KiCad pin header with the plastic on the board top and the long pin up, for the Dupont jumpers of the PDF. The relay board has a 3 pin header and the OLED a 4 pin header, modeled as plastic blocks without metal pins. | Header side and soldering are assumed. The pin tails hang 1.4 mm (DevKit) and 1.8 mm (SuperMini) below the board. A KiCad header is too tall for the relay and OLED boxes, so those two stay as blocks. |
| Plug | What the size limit covers | The limit of 112 x 64 x 46 mm applies to the enclosure body. The pins add 19 mm below it, so the overall height is 65 mm. | The pins cannot fit inside the printed size. The hardware lead must say whether the proposal figure includes them. |
| Plug | Mains interface | Pass-through design. Two male pins on the bottom face (4 mm wide, 19 mm apart, 19 mm long) and two female socket entries of 4.8 mm in the lid. No cable-in design. | Type C and F pin sizes and spacing must be verified before use. The contacts and shutters are not modeled. |
| Plug | Socket sleeves | Open tube under the lid, 10 mm outside width and 12 mm long, directly above each pin at x = -45 mm | Assumed so the socket sits in line with the pins. |
| Plug, Clamp and Clamp meter | Compartments | AC compartment 78 mm long at the -x end, solid partition 1.5 mm thick at x = 25.25 mm, LV compartment 27.5 mm long (Plug) | The partition is 1.5 mm as in the PDF, thinner than the 2.5 mm walls. The wall check leaves it out and a separate check measures it. The PZEM row (31.5 mm) and the relay row (27.5 mm) fill the whole 59 mm inner width. |
| Plug | HLK-PM01, fuse, MOV | HLK-PM01 stands on its short edge (footprint 22 x 17 mm). Fuse is a 5 x 20 mm glass type standing upright. MOV is the Bourns MOV-10D471K, a 12.5 mm disc 7.2 mm thick, standing upright. | Needed to fit the height. The MOV size is from a distributor listing and must be checked on the datasheet. The fuse holder and the wires are not modeled. |
| Plug, Clamp and Clamp meter | ESP32-C3 SuperMini | Turned 90 degrees on two ribs at the +x end. The USB-C port faces the -y side wall and the antenna faces the +y side wall. A 11.5 x 7 mm emergency hole in the -y wall lines up with the port. | The PDF asks for the antenna at an outer wall and a side hole for USB-C. With the board in line with the box the port would face the partition, so the board is turned. The port sits 8.6 mm behind the inside of the wall, because the corner bosses block a closer position. |
| Plug, Clamp and Clamp meter | Ventilation | 5 slots of 20 x 1.8 mm on the +y side wall of the AC compartment, 4.3 mm apart, between the end of the relay and the partition boss | The PDF asks for side vents to remove heat. The relay and PZEM rows leave only small gaps behind the slots, so the airflow is limited. |
| Plug | Wire slot | One slot of 6 x 2 mm in the partition at y = 0 mm, 10 mm above the outside floor face | One slot is too small for all the wires between the compartments. The hardware lead must decide on more slots or a feed-through. |
| Plug | Hanging bosses | Two lid screw bosses at the -x end start 31 mm above the floor, over the PZEM | The PZEM fills the floor there. These bosses need print supports. |
| Plug | Not placed | Two Wago 221-413 connectors (one for L and one for N), an inline fuse holder and the Dupont wires of the PDF | The AC compartment is already full, so they have no space in this layout. The hardware lead must decide how they fit. |
| Clamp | Box size | 107 x 64 x 53.75 mm, derived as wall + AC length + partition + LV length for x, wall + PZEM width + relay width + wall for y | Kept as small as the layout allows. The height follows the lock nut of the PG7 gland. |
| Clamp | PZEM-004T 100 A | Same board size as the v3.0 module (73 x 30 x 24 mm) | The spec gives one size only. |
| Clamp | Split-core CT | CT sensor leads (external) ring, 46 mm outside, 28 mm inside, 30 mm long, on a 4 mm cable | For the render only. It is not part of the STL files. |
| Clamp and Clamp meter | Cable glands | Two real PG7 glands (12.5 mm hole, 17.5 mm lock nut, 23.15 mm long outside the wall) on the -x wall at y = +-12 mm and z = 39 mm. Supply and coil cable placeholder 6 mm wide. | PG7 clamps cables of about 3 to 6.5 mm. A thicker supply cable needs a PG9 gland and a bigger hole. The real gland is bigger than the earlier placeholder, so the box is taller now. The model grips a 2.0 mm wall, so its lock nut is slid 0.7 mm down the thread to clear the 2.5 mm wall. |
| Clamp | Rating | Contactor coil voltage and the 100 A conductor size are not specified | The 10 A relay contacts only drive a contactor coil. Check the coil rating. |
| Clamp meter | Form | Same box, partition, vents, USB hole, parts and glands as the Clamp. A split-core jaw ring (58 mm outside, 33 mm opening, 22 mm thick) is printed onto the -x end of the base, with a 9 mm gap between the jaw tips. | This is the second type of clamp, shaped like the hand held clamp meter of the reference photo. The real jaw hinges and latches. Here only the gap stands for it, and the CT core and its wires are not modeled. The jaw tips are cut flat 3 mm from the outer tip so no thin horn is left. |
| Clamp meter | Size | 107 x 64 x 53.75 mm for the box with the lid, 161 mm long with the jaw | The jaw adds 54 mm to the print length, which still fits the 220 mm build volume. |
| Clamp meter | Jaw position | At the bottom of the -x end wall, with the glands above it | The glands need the upper part of the wall, so the jaw sits low. On a hand held meter the jaw would sit at mid height. |
| Clamp meter | Cables | Gland 1 carries the contactor coil cable and gland 2 the AC supply cable. The CT wires stay inside the jaw. | The PDF sends the CT wires to terminals 3 and 4 of the PZEM. The hole from the jaw into the box for those wires is not modeled. |
| Clamp meter | Display | None | The reference photo shows a display. The PDF gives the Clamp no display, so none is added. |
| Hub | ESP32 DevKit V1 | 38-pin board of 51 x 28 x 13 mm | Not in the spec. The real board must be measured. |
| Hub | OLED | PCB 27 x 27 mm, 4 mm thick with the header, window 24 x 13 mm centred over the PCB, held by four 2.5 mm blocks | The glass area of real modules is offset on the PCB. Check the window position. No clip, glue or screw is modeled. |
| Hub | USB opening | 12 x 7 mm on the -x end wall, connector 3.1 mm above the board bottom | The connector height is a guess. |
| Hub | Ventilation | 5 slots of 20 x 1.8 mm on the +x end wall, 4.3 mm apart | The brief asks for a slot. Five slots keep a 2.5 mm bridge between them. |

## Not covered

- Creepage and clearance distances for mains, insulation class, flame rating of the plastic and any approval (SNI, IEC).
- Heat build-up, strength of the printed parts, print supports and warping.
- Real dimensions of the purchased modules, which can differ from the spec.
- Antenna range through the enclosure wall.
